## Notes

Human Longevity Inc. (HLI) was founded in 2013 by genomics pioneer J. Craig Venter with the mission to extend healthy human lifespan using genomics, advanced diagnostics, and machine learning. The company offers comprehensive health assessments called "Health Nucleus" that combine whole genome sequencing, full-body MRI, advanced biomarkers, and AI-powered analysis.

The company built one of the largest databases of whole human genomes combined with phenotype data. HLI's approach aims to detect diseases at their earliest stages when interventions are most effective, particularly for conditions like cancer, cardiovascular disease, and metabolic disorders.

HLI has undergone management changes since its founding, with Venter stepping back from day-to-day operations. The company has pivoted more toward executive health assessments and concierge medicine.
