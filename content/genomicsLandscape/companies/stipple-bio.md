## Notes

Stipple Bio is a biotechnology company focused on unlocking cellular profiling and target discovery with epitope resolution. The company uses proprietary epitope-level resolution technology to enable single-cell profiling and target discovery.

The company's technology provides unprecedented resolution in understanding protein expression at the single-cell level, enabling:
- Identification of novel drug targets
- Understanding of cellular heterogeneity
- Epitope-level characterization of protein expression
- Applications in drug discovery and development

By providing epitope-level resolution, Stipple Bio's platform can reveal subtle differences in protein expression and conformation that traditional methods miss.
