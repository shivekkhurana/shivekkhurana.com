## Notes

Paratek Pharmaceuticals is a biopharmaceutical company focused on developing and commercializing novel antibiotics for serious bacterial infections. The company's lead product, NUZYRA (omadacycline), is an aminomethylcycline antibiotic designed to overcome common bacterial resistance mechanisms.

Paratek addresses the critical public health challenge of antimicrobial resistance by developing new antibiotics active against resistant pathogens.
