Evaro is a digital health company providing embedded pharmacy solutions that allow businesses to integrate pharmacy services directly into their platforms. This B2B2C model enables healthcare providers, telehealth platforms, and other businesses to offer prescription fulfillment as part of their customer experience.

The embedded pharmacy model represents a growing trend in healthcare, where pharmacy services are integrated into digital health platforms to improve convenience and patient adherence to medications.
