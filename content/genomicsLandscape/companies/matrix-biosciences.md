## Notes

Matrix Biosciences is a pioneering biotechnology company and the first spinout from VitaDAO, the decentralized autonomous organization (DAO) focused on funding longevity research. Launched in October 2023 in collaboration with Dr. Vera Gorbunova from the University of Rochester's Aging Research Center, the company focuses on developing treatments for cancer and aging-related disorders.

The company's approach centers on developing small molecules that modulate hyaluronidases, enzymes involved in breaking down hyaluronic acid, with potential applications in cancer treatment and extending human healthspan and lifespan.
