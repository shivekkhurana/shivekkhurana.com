## Notes

Noema Pharma is a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company focused on developing novel treatments for neurological and psychiatric conditions with significant unmet medical need. The company's pipeline includes therapies for tinnitus, autism spectrum disorder (ASD), and treatment-resistant depression.

Based in Basel, Switzerland, Noema leverages deep expertise in neuroscience and psychiatry to advance differentiated therapies targeting the nervous system.
