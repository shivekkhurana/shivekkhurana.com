## Notes

CereVasc is a medical device company developing novel minimally invasive treatments for hydrocephalus, a debilitating neurological condition affecting over one million patients in the United States. The company's eShunt™ System is designed to provide a permanent, minimally invasive solution for cerebrospinal fluid (CSF) drainage.

The eShunt System is an endovascular implant that creates a new pathway for CSF drainage, potentially eliminating the need for traditional surgical shunts that have high failure rates and require multiple revisions.
