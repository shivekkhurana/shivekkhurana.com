## Notes

Moonlight Therapeutics is a biotechnology company focused on developing novel immunotherapy treatments for peanut allergy. The company is working to address the significant unmet need for safe and effective therapies for the millions of people living with potentially life-threatening peanut allergies.

Spun out of Texas Tech University, Moonlight Therapeutics is developing pre-clinical stage approaches that aim to desensitize the immune system to peanut allergens, potentially providing long-lasting protection against allergic reactions.
