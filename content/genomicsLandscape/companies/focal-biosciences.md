## Notes

Focal Biosciences is a biotechnology company focused on developing epigenetic modulators for cellular reprogramming. The company aims to harness the power of epigenetic modifications to reprogram cells for therapeutic applications in regenerative medicine.

The company operates in stealth mode with limited public information available. Their work involves developing small molecules and other approaches that can modify the epigenetic state of cells, potentially enabling cell fate transitions for therapeutic purposes.

Focal Biosciences is backed by Apollo Health Ventures, a venture capital firm focused on longevity and aging-related biotechnology investments.
