## Notes

Kailera Therapeutics is a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company focused on developing next-generation therapies for obesity and related metabolic conditions. The company launched in October 2024 with an industry-leading $400 million Series A financing, one of the largest Series A rounds in biotech history.

Kailera's lead program KAI-9531 (developed in China as HRS9531) is an injectable dual GLP-1/GIP receptor agonist that has demonstrated positive clinical trial results in obesity and type 2 diabetes. The company is also developing oral therapies for weight management.

In November 2025, the company announced a $600 million Series B financing to further advance its pipeline.
