## Notes

CS Genetics is a Cambridge, UK-based biotechnology company developing a disruptive platform for single-cell genomics. The company's technology leverages instrument-free, solution-phase chemical biology, which offers potential advantages in scalability and accessibility compared to traditional microfluidics-based approaches.

The company's approach aims to democratize single-cell analysis by removing the need for specialized instrumentation, potentially making the technology more accessible to a broader range of research laboratories and clinical applications.
