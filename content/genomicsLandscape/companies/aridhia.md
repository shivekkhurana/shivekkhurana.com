## Notes

Aridhia Informatics is a Scottish health technology company that provides secure, cloud-based platforms for collaborative healthcare research and data analytics. The company's flagship product, AnalytiXagility, is a trusted research environment (TRE) that enables researchers to securely analyze sensitive health data while maintaining privacy and governance standards.

Aridhia works with healthcare systems, research institutions, and life sciences companies to support precision medicine initiatives, clinical trials, and population health research. The platform enables federated analytics and collaborative research across multiple data sources while maintaining data security and regulatory compliance.

The company has been a key technology partner for several major UK health data initiatives and genomics programs.
