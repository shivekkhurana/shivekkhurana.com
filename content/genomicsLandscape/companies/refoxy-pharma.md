## Notes

Refoxy Pharma is a biotechnology company based in Berlin, Germany, focused on developing therapeutics that modulate stress response pathways to promote healthy aging and longevity. The company's approach targets the body's natural stress response mechanisms, particularly oxidative stress pathways, to develop treatments for age-related diseases.

The company is part of the Apollo Health Ventures portfolio, which focuses on longevity and aging-related biotechnology investments.
