## Notes

Actym Therapeutics is a biotechnology company pioneering the use of engineered bacteria for cancer therapy. Their proprietary STACT (Systemically-administered Tumor-homing Actym Construct Technology) platform uses programmed bacteria to selectively colonize solid tumors and deliver therapeutic payloads directly to the tumor microenvironment.

The company's approach enables systemic delivery with tumor-specific effects, potentially overcoming limitations of traditional drug delivery methods for solid tumors.
