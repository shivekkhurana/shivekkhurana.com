## Notes

Ultima Genomics is a gene sequencing company focused on dramatically reducing the cost of genome sequencing. The company is headquartered in Fremont, California.

Ultima exited stealth mode in June 2022 with approximately $600 million in venture capital funding and an ambitious goal: to reduce the cost of sequencing a human genome to $100. The company's novel sequencing approach uses a flow-based technology that differs from existing sequencing platforms.

Key innovations include:
- Novel sequencing chemistry
- High-throughput flow cell design
- Cost-efficient reagent consumption
- Scalable architecture

The $100 genome would represent a significant milestone in making genomic sequencing accessible for routine clinical use.
