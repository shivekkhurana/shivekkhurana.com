## Notes

Viracta Therapeutics is a precision oncology company developing targeted therapies for virus-associated cancers. The company is publicly traded on NASDAQ under the ticker symbol VIRX and is headquartered in San Diego, California.

The company focuses on cancers associated with viral infections, particularly Epstein-Barr virus (EBV). EBV is associated with several types of cancer, including:
- Certain lymphomas
- Nasopharyngeal carcinoma
- Gastric cancer

Viracta's approach leverages the unique biology of virus-associated cancers to develop targeted therapies that are more effective and less toxic than conventional chemotherapy.
