## Notes

Cabaletta Bio (NASDAQ: CABA) is a clinical-stage biotechnology company pioneering the development of engineered T cell therapies for autoimmune diseases. The company's CAAR (Chimeric AutoAntibody Receptor) T cell platform is designed to selectively target and eliminate B cells that produce disease-causing autoantibodies.

Founded in 2017, Cabaletta Bio is developing treatments for conditions such as mucosal pemphigus vulgaris (mPV) and other autoimmune diseases. The company went public in 2020 and is headquartered in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania.
