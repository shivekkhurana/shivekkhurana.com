## Notes

Cellworks is a computational biology company leveraging artificial intelligence and machine learning to accelerate drug discovery and enable personalized therapy selection. Their platform creates digital twins of patient tumors using genomic data, enabling in silico simulation of drug responses.

The company's Cellworks Singula platform helps oncologists identify optimal treatment strategies for individual cancer patients, while their drug discovery platform accelerates the identification and validation of novel therapeutic targets.
