## Notes

Palvella Therapeutics is a clinical-stage biotechnology company focused on developing novel topical therapies for rare and serious skin diseases. The company's lead program targets pachyonychia congenita (PC), a rare genetic skin disorder, and other dermatological conditions with significant unmet need.

Palvella's approach leverages proprietary drug delivery technology to enable effective topical treatments for conditions that currently lack adequate therapies.
