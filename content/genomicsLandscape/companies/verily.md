## Notes

Verily Life Sciences is Alphabet's (Google's parent company) life sciences and healthcare company. Formerly known as Google Life Sciences, Verily was established as an independent Alphabet subsidiary in 2015 and is headquartered in South San Francisco, California.

Verily focuses on precision health, combining advanced technology, data science, and healthcare expertise to:
- Develop innovative medical devices
- Create digital health tools and platforms
- Enable clinical research and drug development
- Generate real-world evidence
- Improve population health management

The company has partnerships with major pharmaceutical companies and healthcare organizations to advance precision medicine and improve health outcomes.
