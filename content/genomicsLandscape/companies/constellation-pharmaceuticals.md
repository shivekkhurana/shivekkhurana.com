## Notes

Constellation Pharmaceuticals was a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company focused on developing novel therapeutics that target gene expression through epigenetic modulation. The company specialized in developing treatments for hematological malignancies and solid tumors.

In July 2021, MorphoSys AG acquired Constellation Pharmaceuticals for approximately $1.7 billion. The acquisition brought pelabresib, a BET inhibitor being developed for myelofibrosis, into MorphoSys's pipeline.
