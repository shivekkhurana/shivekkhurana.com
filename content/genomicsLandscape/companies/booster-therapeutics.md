## Notes

Booster Therapeutics is a biotechnology company committed to developing innovative therapies that target the accumulation of toxic cellular materials associated with degenerative diseases and aging. The company's approach focuses on restoring cellular homeostasis by eliminating harmful accumulations that disrupt normal cell function.

Booster Therapeutics launched in October 2024 with $15 million in seed financing led by Apollo Health Ventures and Novo Holdings, two leading life sciences investors with strong track records in longevity and biotechnology investments.
