## Notes

Alkeus Pharmaceuticals is a clinical-stage biotechnology company focused on developing treatments for Stargardt disease and other forms of inherited retinal degenerations. The company's lead candidate, gildeuretinol (ALK-001), is a modified vitamin A designed to slow the accumulation of toxic vitamin A dimers (A2E) in the retinal pigment epithelium.

Stargardt disease is the most common form of inherited juvenile macular degeneration, affecting approximately 1 in 10,000 people worldwide. ALK-001 has received FDA Orphan Drug and Fast Track designations.
