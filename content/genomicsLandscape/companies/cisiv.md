## Notes

CISIV is developing intelligent technology solutions for decentralized research and late-stage clinical trials. Their platform enables sponsors and CROs to conduct hybrid and fully decentralized trials, allowing patients to participate from their homes while maintaining data quality and regulatory compliance.

The company's technology addresses the growing demand for more accessible and patient-centric clinical trial models, accelerated by the COVID-19 pandemic's impact on traditional site-based research.
