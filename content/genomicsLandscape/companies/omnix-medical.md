## Notes

Omnix Medical is a medical device company focused on developing innovative antimicrobial solutions to prevent and treat infections associated with medical devices and wounds. The company's proprietary Omniguard platform provides broad-spectrum antimicrobial protection.

Omnix addresses the critical problem of healthcare-associated infections (HAIs), which pose significant risks to patients and create substantial costs for healthcare systems.
