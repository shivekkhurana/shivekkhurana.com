## Notes

Disc Medicine is a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company headquartered in Watertown, Massachusetts, focused on developing treatments for serious hematologic diseases. The company targets fundamental biological pathways that regulate red blood cell production, including iron metabolism and erythropoiesis.

The company's lead programs target conditions where patients have ineffective red blood cell production, such as beta-thalassemia, polycythemia vera, and myelofibrosis. Disc Medicine is publicly traded on NASDAQ under the ticker IRON.
