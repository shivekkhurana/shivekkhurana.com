## Notes

Amecath is a leading Egyptian medical device manufacturer specializing in cardiovascular catheters and interventional cardiology products. The company serves hospitals and healthcare providers across the Middle East and North Africa region, providing affordable alternatives to imported medical devices.

Backed by TVM Capital Healthcare, Amecath is focused on expanding its product portfolio and regional presence to address the growing cardiovascular disease burden in emerging markets.
