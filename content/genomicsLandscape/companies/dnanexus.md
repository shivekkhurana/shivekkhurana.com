## Notes

DNAnexus is a leading cloud-based platform for genomic and biomedical data management and analysis, headquartered in Mountain View, California. The company provides a secure, compliant environment for storing and analyzing large-scale genomic datasets, supporting both research and clinical applications.

The platform enables researchers and clinicians to store, share, and analyze DNA sequence data at scale, with built-in compliance for regulatory requirements including HIPAA, GDPR, and GxP. DNAnexus has become a critical infrastructure provider for large-scale genomics initiatives and pharmaceutical research programs.

The company has partnered with major healthcare organizations, pharmaceutical companies, and research consortiums to power their genomics data infrastructure, including UK Biobank and various pharmaceutical drug discovery programs.
