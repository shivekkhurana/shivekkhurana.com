## Notes

IVIEW Therapeutics is a biotechnology company focused on developing novel therapeutics for ocular diseases. The company is working on treatments for conditions affecting the retina and other parts of the eye, addressing significant unmet medical needs in ophthalmology.

Eye diseases including age-related macular degeneration, diabetic retinopathy, and glaucoma affect millions of patients globally, creating significant demand for new therapeutic approaches.

IVIEW Therapeutics is backed by BioAdvance, a life sciences investment organization focused on early-stage companies in the Mid-Atlantic region.
