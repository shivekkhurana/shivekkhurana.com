## Notes

Avillion is a London-based pharmaceutical co-development company that provides a unique model for drug development partnerships. The company partners with pharmaceutical and biotechnology companies to share the risk and cost of clinical development, providing both capital and operational expertise to advance drug programs through regulatory approval.

Avillion's innovative financing model allows pharma partners to maintain optionality while accessing non-dilutive capital and development capabilities, making it an attractive partner for companies looking to advance their pipelines efficiently.
