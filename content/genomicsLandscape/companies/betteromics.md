## Notes

Betteromics is a digital medicine company that has developed a certified SaaS platform for integrating and analyzing health and omics data. The platform is designed to unify disparate data sources - including genomics, proteomics, metabolomics, and clinical data - into a single, actionable source of truth for precision medicine applications.

The company serves healthcare providers, pharmaceutical companies, and research institutions looking to leverage multi-omics data for clinical decision-making, drug development, and personalized treatment strategies.
