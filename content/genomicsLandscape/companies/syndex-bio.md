## Notes

Syndex Bio is a biotechnology company founded in 2022, specializing in the development of genomics-based tools for biological discovery. The company integrates molecular technologies and instrumentation to enable novel approaches to drug discovery and biological research.

The company's platform combines advances in genomics, molecular biology, and instrumentation to provide researchers with new tools for understanding biological systems and discovering new therapeutic targets.
