## Notes

FOLX Health is a virtual healthcare company focused on providing accessible, affirming healthcare for the LGBTQIA+ community. The platform offers hormone therapy (HRT) for transgender and non-binary individuals, sexual health services including PrEP, and mental health support.

The company was founded in 2019 and has raised significant venture capital to expand its services. FOLX differentiates itself by providing clinician-led care specifically designed for LGBTQ+ patients, with a membership model that includes unlimited messaging with providers and home delivery of medications.

FOLX has faced challenges common to telehealth companies including regulatory complexity across states and the need to build trust within the LGBTQ+ community for sensitive healthcare services.
