## Notes

Kyverna Therapeutics is a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company focused on developing CAR-T cell therapies for autoimmune diseases. The company is pioneering the application of chimeric antigen receptor (CAR) T-cell technology, originally developed for cancer, to treat severe autoimmune conditions by targeting and eliminating autoreactive B cells.

The company completed its IPO in February 2024 on NASDAQ under the ticker KYTX. Kyverna's lead product candidate, KYV-101, targets CD19 and is being developed for multiple autoimmune indications.
