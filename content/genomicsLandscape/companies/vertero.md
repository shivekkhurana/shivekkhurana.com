## Notes

Vertero is a biotechnology company focused on systems biology approaches to drug discovery and development. The company is headquartered in Boston, Massachusetts.

Systems biology combines computational modeling with experimental biology to understand complex biological systems as integrated networks rather than isolated components. This approach can reveal:
- Novel drug targets
- Disease mechanisms
- Biomarkers for patient selection
- Combination therapy opportunities

Vertero is a portfolio company of Corundum Systems Biology.
