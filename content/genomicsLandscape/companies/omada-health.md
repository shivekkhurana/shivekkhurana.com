## Notes

Omada Health is a leading digital health company that empowers people to achieve their health goals through sustainable lifestyle change. The company offers virtual-first care programs for chronic disease prevention and management, including diabetes prevention, weight management, musculoskeletal care, and behavioral health.

Omada's platform combines personalized coaching with connected devices and data analytics to deliver evidence-based care at scale.
