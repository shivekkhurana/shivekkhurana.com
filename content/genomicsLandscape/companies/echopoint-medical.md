## Notes

Echopoint Medical is a UK-based medical device company focused on developing optical fiber-based sensor technology for interventional cardiology. The company's technology aims to provide cardiologists with enhanced imaging and measurement capabilities during interventional procedures.

Optical fiber sensors offer potential advantages in interventional cardiology, including miniaturization, real-time feedback, and the ability to provide multiple measurements from a single catheter. These technologies could improve procedural outcomes and reduce complications in cardiac interventions.
