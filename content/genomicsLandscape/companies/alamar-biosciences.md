## Notes

Alamar Biosciences is a biotechnology company revolutionizing proteomics with its proprietary NULISA (NUcleic acid-Linked Immuno-Sandwich Assay) platform. The technology enables ultra-sensitive and highly multiplexed protein detection from minimal sample volumes, addressing limitations of traditional immunoassays.

The NULISA platform combines antibody-based protein capture with DNA-based amplification and detection, achieving attomolar sensitivity while maintaining high specificity. Applications span drug development, biomarker discovery, and clinical diagnostics.
