## Notes

MOMA Therapeutics is a biopharmaceutical company focused on developing precision medicines that target molecular machines—the protein complexes that power essential cellular processes. The company's approach enables it to drug previously undruggable targets by understanding and exploiting the unique biology of these dynamic molecular machines.

MOMA's platform integrates structural biology, biochemistry, and medicinal chemistry to identify and develop novel therapeutics against molecular machines implicated in cancer and other serious diseases.
