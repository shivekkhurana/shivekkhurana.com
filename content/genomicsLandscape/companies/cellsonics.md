## Notes

Cellsonics is a biotechnology company developing proprietary SimpleFlow™ technology to enable the first fully automated tissue dissociation workflow from solid tissue to single cells. Their technology maintains native gene expression during the dissociation process, addressing a critical challenge in single-cell genomics and research.

The company's automated workflow reduces variability and improves reproducibility in single-cell analysis, making it easier for researchers to obtain high-quality single-cell data from tissue samples.
