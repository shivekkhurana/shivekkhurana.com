## Notes

deepc is a Munich-based digital health company providing an AI platform for radiology. The company offers a comprehensive solution that enables healthcare providers to integrate, deploy, and manage multiple AI applications across their radiology workflows through a single platform.

The deepc platform addresses key challenges in adopting AI in healthcare by providing a vendor-agnostic marketplace of AI algorithms, seamless integration with existing PACS systems, and tools for monitoring and validating AI performance in clinical settings.

As part of the Sofinnova Partners Digital Medicine portfolio, deepc represents the growing intersection of artificial intelligence and medical imaging, a field with significant potential to improve diagnostic accuracy and workflow efficiency.
