## Notes

Mitsubishi Tanabe Pharma is one of Japan's oldest and largest pharmaceutical companies, with origins dating back to 1678. The company is a subsidiary of Mitsubishi Chemical Holdings Corporation and focuses on developing innovative medicines in areas including central nervous system disorders, autoimmune diseases, and diabetes.

The company is known for developing Radicava (edaravone), an FDA-approved treatment for amyotrophic lateral sclerosis (ALS), and has a diverse pipeline of therapeutics in various stages of development.
