## Notes

Elevara Medicine is a clinical-stage biotechnology company focused on developing innovative therapies for rheumatoid arthritis and other chronic inflammatory disorders. The company is part of the Sofinnova Partners portfolio.

Rheumatoid arthritis and other inflammatory conditions represent significant unmet medical needs, affecting millions of patients worldwide. Despite existing treatments, many patients do not achieve adequate disease control, creating opportunities for new therapeutic approaches.
