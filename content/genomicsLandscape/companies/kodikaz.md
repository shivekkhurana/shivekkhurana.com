## Notes

Kodikaz Therapeutic Solutions is a biotechnology company focused on developing viral vector manufacturing capabilities and cell-specific therapeutic delivery platforms for gene therapy applications. The company works to advance gene therapy by improving the production and targeting of viral vectors used in genetic medicine.

Based in Orlando, Florida, Kodikaz is part of the growing gene therapy manufacturing ecosystem addressing the industry's need for scalable, high-quality vector production.
