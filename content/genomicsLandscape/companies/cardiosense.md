## Notes

Cardiosense is a clinical-stage digital health company developing AI-powered wearable technology for non-invasive cardiac monitoring, particularly focused on congestive heart failure. The company's technology enables early detection of heart failure decompensation, allowing for proactive intervention before patients require hospitalization.

Cardiosense spun out of research from Georgia Tech University and Northwestern University, combining expertise in biomedical engineering and artificial intelligence.
