## Notes

Twist Bioscience is a synthetic biology company that provides synthetic DNA-based products for various industries including healthcare, agriculture, industrial chemicals, and data storage. The company is publicly traded on NASDAQ under the ticker symbol TWST and is headquartered in South San Francisco, California.

Twist's proprietary silicon-based DNA synthesis platform enables high-throughput, low-cost production of synthetic DNA. The company's products and services include:
- Gene synthesis
- Oligo pools
- NGS target enrichment
- Antibody discovery
- DNA data storage
- Biopharma services

The company's technology has enabled significant cost reductions in DNA synthesis, making synthetic biology more accessible to researchers and companies.
