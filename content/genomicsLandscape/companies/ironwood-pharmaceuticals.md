## Notes

Ironwood Pharmaceuticals is a commercial-stage biopharmaceutical company focused on developing and commercializing treatments for gastrointestinal (GI) diseases. The company's lead product LINZESS (linaclotide) is a first-in-class guanylate cyclase-C agonist approved for irritable bowel syndrome with constipation (IBS-C) and chronic idiopathic constipation (CIC).

Founded in 1998 and publicly traded on NASDAQ (ticker: IRWD), Ironwood has built a significant commercial presence in the GI space. LINZESS is co-developed and co-promoted with AbbVie (formerly Allergan) in the United States.

The company continues to develop new products for GI conditions and has expanded its focus to include other areas where guanylate cyclase biology may be relevant.
