## Notes

BioCorteX is a digital medicine company that combines artificial intelligence with computational biology to advance precision oncology. The company's platform analyzes complex biological data to identify novel drug targets, predict treatment responses, and develop more effective cancer therapies.

By integrating multi-omics data with clinical information, BioCorteX aims to accelerate drug discovery and enable more personalized treatment approaches for cancer patients.
