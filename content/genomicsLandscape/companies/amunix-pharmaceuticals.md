## Notes

Amunix Pharmaceuticals was a biotechnology company that developed innovative protein engineering platforms for creating next-generation biologics. The company's proprietary XTEN technology enabled the extension of half-life and improvement of drug properties, while its Pro-XTEN platform created protease-activated prodrugs that activate specifically in the tumor microenvironment.

**Acquired by Sanofi in August 2021** for $1 billion upfront plus up to $225 million in milestone payments. The acquisition provided Sanofi with Amunix's pipeline of masked T cell engagers and its XTEN/Pro-XTEN platforms.
