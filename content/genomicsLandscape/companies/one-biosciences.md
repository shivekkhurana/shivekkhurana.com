## Notes

One Biosciences is a biotechnology company that leverages single-cell technologies combined with artificial intelligence and machine learning to identify novel therapeutic targets and develop new medicines for difficult-to-treat diseases.

The company's platform enables deep biological insights at the single-cell level, allowing for better understanding of disease mechanisms and identification of new drug targets.
