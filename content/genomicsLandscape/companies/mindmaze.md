## Notes

MindMaze is a digital health and neurotechnology company developing breakthrough platforms for brain health and recovery. The company combines virtual reality (VR), artificial intelligence, motion capture, and brain-computer interfaces to create immersive therapies for patients recovering from stroke, traumatic brain injury, and other neurological conditions.

Founded in 2012 and headquartered in Lausanne, Switzerland, MindMaze has achieved unicorn status with valuations exceeding $1 billion. The company's platforms are used in hospitals and rehabilitation centers worldwide to accelerate patient recovery through engaging, gamified therapeutic experiences.
