## Notes

Turn Biotechnologies (Turn.Bio) is a biotechnology company developing mRNA medicines that instruct specific cells to fight disease or repair damaged tissue. The company is headquartered in Mountain View, California.

Turn's proprietary ERA (Epigenetic Reprogramming of Aging) technology uses mRNA therapeutics delivered via lipid nanoparticles to reprogram the epigenome and restore cellular functions lost with age. This approach aims to:
- Reverse cellular aging
- Restore youthful gene expression patterns
- Rejuvenate aged tissues
- Treat age-related diseases

The company has received funding from VitaDAO, a decentralized autonomous organization focused on longevity research.
