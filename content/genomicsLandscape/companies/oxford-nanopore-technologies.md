## Notes

Oxford Nanopore Technologies is a pioneering life sciences company that has developed a new generation of nanopore-based sensing technology for DNA and RNA sequencing. The company's technology enables real-time, long-read sequencing in devices ranging from portable pocket-sized sequencers (MinION) to high-throughput systems (PromethION).

Oxford Nanopore's technology is unique in its ability to sequence native DNA and RNA without amplification, providing real-time results and enabling applications from research to clinical diagnostics and point-of-care testing.
