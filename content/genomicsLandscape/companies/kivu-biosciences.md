## Notes

Kivu Biosciences is a biotechnology company focused on developing treatments for neurodegenerative diseases. The company is working to address the significant unmet medical need in conditions such as Alzheimer's disease, Parkinson's disease, and other neurological disorders that currently lack effective disease-modifying treatments.

Backed by Novo Holdings, Kivu Biosciences is pursuing novel approaches to tackle the complex biology underlying neurodegenerative conditions.
