## Notes

Elephant Healthcare is a digital health company focused on improving healthcare delivery in Africa. The company provides electronic medical record (EMR) and practice management software to clinics and hospitals, enabling better patient care, clinical decision support, and healthcare data management.

Operating primarily in East Africa, Elephant Healthcare addresses the critical need for digital healthcare infrastructure in underserved markets. The company's platform helps healthcare providers manage patient records, appointments, billing, and inventory while providing clinical decision support tools.

Elephant Healthcare is part of the Entree Capital portfolio, reflecting investor interest in digital health solutions for emerging markets.
