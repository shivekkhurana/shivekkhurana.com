## Notes

Scripta Therapeutics is a biotechnology company focused on developing longevity therapeutics. The company is working on novel approaches to address age-related diseases through epigenetic and cellular reprogramming technologies.

In November 2025, Scripta Therapeutics raised $12 million in seed funding to advance its longevity therapeutics programs. The company is backed by Apollo Health Ventures, which specializes in longevity and aging-related biotechnology investments.
