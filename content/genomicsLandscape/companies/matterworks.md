## Notes

Matterworks is building a machine-learning software platform designed to transform mass spectrometry (MS) analysis. The company's technology enables users of MS instruments to avoid chromatography steps when analyzing metabolomic samples, dramatically reducing analysis time while maintaining accuracy.

By applying advanced AI and machine learning to mass spectrometry data, Matterworks aims to make metabolomics research faster, more accessible, and more cost-effective for pharmaceutical development, diagnostics, and life sciences research.
