## Notes

NEUmiRNA Therapeutics is a biotechnology company focused on developing microRNA (miRNA)-based therapeutics. The company's proprietary platform enables the development of miRNA modulators for treating various diseases, with a focus on neurodegenerative conditions.

MicroRNAs are small non-coding RNA molecules that regulate gene expression, and modulating specific miRNAs offers a novel approach to treating diseases at the genetic level.
