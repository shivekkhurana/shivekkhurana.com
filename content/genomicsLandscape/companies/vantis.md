## Notes

VANTIS is a Hybrid Care Platform company focused on innovative healthcare delivery. The company combines digital health solutions with traditional in-person care to create a more accessible and effective healthcare experience.

The hybrid care model aims to:
- Improve patient access to healthcare
- Reduce healthcare costs
- Enhance care coordination
- Enable continuous patient monitoring
- Optimize the balance between virtual and in-person care

VANTIS is a portfolio company of Angelini Ventures (2024 investment).
