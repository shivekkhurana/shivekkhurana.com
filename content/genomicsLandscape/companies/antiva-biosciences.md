## Notes

Antiva Biosciences is a clinical-stage biotechnology company developing novel non-surgical therapeutic candidates to treat pre-cancerous cervical lesions caused by high-risk human papillomavirus (HPV). The company's approach aims to provide an alternative to surgical procedures like LEEP (loop electrosurgical excision procedure) for treating cervical dysplasia.

The lead candidate ATV-2000 is designed to eliminate HPV-infected cells while preserving normal cervical tissue, potentially offering a less invasive treatment option for women with cervical intraepithelial neoplasia (CIN).
