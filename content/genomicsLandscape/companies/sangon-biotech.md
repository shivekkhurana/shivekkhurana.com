## Notes

Sangon Biotech is one of China's leading providers of life science tools and services. Founded in 2001 and headquartered in Shanghai, the company has grown to serve a vast customer base including over 7,000 academic institutions and 40,000 biotech companies across China.

The company offers a comprehensive range of products and services including:
- DNA/RNA synthesis and oligo manufacturing
- R&D reagents and consumables
- Genetic sequencing services
- Protein-related products and services
- Custom biology services

In August 2023, Sangon Biotech raised $290 million in strategic financing, with Novo Holdings participating as an investor, highlighting the company's strong position in the Chinese life sciences market.
