## Notes

Synaptrix is a medical device company developing electrical stimulation technology for pain relief in patients undergoing total knee arthroplasty (TKA). The company is headquartered in Atlanta, Georgia.

The company's technology provides non-opioid pain management solutions for orthopedic surgery patients, addressing the significant need for alternatives to opioid-based pain management in post-surgical care. Electrical stimulation can help reduce pain, improve recovery, and decrease reliance on opioid medications.

Synaptrix is a portfolio company of Portal Innovations and is currently in clinical stage development. The company spun out of Avanos Medical.
