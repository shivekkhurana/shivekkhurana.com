## Notes

Parsley Health is a virtual-first primary care practice that combines functional medicine with conventional medical care. The company's approach focuses on identifying and treating the root causes of chronic health conditions rather than just managing symptoms.

Parsley Health serves patients with complex chronic conditions including autoimmune disorders, digestive issues, hormonal imbalances, and metabolic conditions through a membership model that provides extended doctor visits, health coaching, and personalized care plans.
