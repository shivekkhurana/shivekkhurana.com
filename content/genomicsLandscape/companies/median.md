## Notes

Median Technologies is a healthcare technology company specializing in AI-powered medical imaging solutions. The company provides services to pharmaceutical companies conducting oncology clinical trials and is developing AI-based diagnostic products for early cancer detection.

Listed on Euronext Growth Paris, Median has established itself as a leader in imaging analysis for clinical trials, particularly in oncology, while also advancing its iBiopsy® platform for early cancer diagnosis using AI and radiomics.
