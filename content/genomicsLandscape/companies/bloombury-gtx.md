## Notes

Bloomsbury Genetic Therapies (Bloomsbury GTX) is a UK-based biotechnology company focused on developing gene therapies for rare neurological and metabolic diseases. The company aims to provide potentially curative one-time treatments for patients who currently have limited or no treatment options.

The company's pipeline targets devastating rare diseases where gene therapy has the potential to address the underlying genetic cause and transform patient outcomes. Bloomsbury GTX leverages advanced gene therapy vectors and delivery technologies to develop safe and effective treatments.
