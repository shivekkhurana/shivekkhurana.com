## Notes

Rebus Biosystems is illuminating the spectrum of spatial discovery by developing spatial biology platforms for genomics research. The company's technology enables researchers to analyze gene expression in intact tissue samples, preserving spatial context that is lost in traditional single-cell methods.

Rebus's platform provides high-throughput, high-resolution spatial genomics analysis to accelerate research in oncology, neuroscience, and other fields.
