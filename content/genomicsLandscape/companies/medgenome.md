## Notes

MedGenome is one of the largest genomics companies in South Asia, providing comprehensive genetic testing and research services. The company offers a wide range of genomic tests for clinical diagnostics, including whole genome sequencing, whole exome sequencing, and targeted panels for various diseases.

With operations in India, the US, and expansion into Africa and the Middle East, MedGenome serves both clinical laboratories seeking diagnostic solutions and pharmaceutical companies requiring genomic research services. The company has built one of the largest South Asian genomic databases, providing unique insights into genetic variants prevalent in these populations.
