## Notes

NOCD is the leading digital health company focused on providing accessible, affordable, and effective treatment for obsessive-compulsive disorder (OCD). The company connects patients with licensed therapists who specialize in exposure and response prevention (ERP), the gold standard treatment for OCD.

The platform offers virtual therapy sessions, an in-app peer community, and tools to help patients manage symptoms between sessions. NOCD works with major insurance plans to make treatment accessible.

- Founded in 2014 in Chicago by Stephen Smith, who experienced OCD firsthand

- Partners with major health insurance plans for coverage
- Raised over $90 million in funding from investors including 7wire Ventures, F-Prime Capital
- ERP therapy via telehealth has shown comparable outcomes to in-person treatment
- Focus on reducing wait times - average of less than 2 weeks to start treatment
- Also provides OCD assessments and resources for family members
