## Notes

Neumora Therapeutics is a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company focused on developing precision medicines for patients with brain diseases. The company combines proprietary computational neuroscience, deep patient phenotyping, and clinical development to advance novel therapies for psychiatric and neurological disorders including major depressive disorder (MDD), generalized anxiety disorder (GAD), and other conditions.

Founded in 2019, Neumora went public on NASDAQ (ticker: NMRA) in 2023. The company was originally known as RBNC Therapeutics before rebranding to Neumora.
