## Notes

Benchling is a leading life sciences R&D cloud platform that has transformed how scientists manage their research workflows. The platform provides integrated tools for molecular biology, including sequence design, CRISPR guide design, and plasmid management, along with electronic lab notebooks (ELN), sample management, and inventory tracking.

The company has grown rapidly to become the standard R&D platform for many of the world's leading biotech and pharmaceutical companies, with over 200,000 scientists using the platform globally. Benchling raised a significant Series F funding round with participation from Altimeter Capital.
