## Notes

ArriVent BioPharma is a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company focused on developing precision cancer therapies for patients with genetically defined tumors. The company's lead program, furmonertinib, is a third-generation EGFR tyrosine kinase inhibitor (TKI) for the treatment of non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC) with EGFR mutations.

ArriVent's strategy focuses on bringing innovative cancer therapies that have shown promise in other markets to patients in the United States and globally. The company has secured rights to develop and commercialize therapies for specific geographic regions.

The company went public in 2024 and is traded on NASDAQ. ArriVent is backed by leading healthcare investors including Novo Holdings.
