## Notes

Turbine is an AI-powered drug discovery company using simulation biology to predict how cancer cells respond to treatments. The company is headquartered in Budapest, Hungary.

Turbine's platform uses AI and computational modeling to simulate cancer cell behavior at the molecular level, enabling:
- Prediction of drug responses
- Identification of novel drug combinations
- Understanding of resistance mechanisms
- Biomarker discovery
- Patient stratification

The company's simulation-based approach allows for rapid hypothesis testing and drug candidate prioritization, potentially accelerating the drug discovery process.
