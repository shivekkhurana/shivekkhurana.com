## Notes

Assurex Health was a pioneering genetic testing company focused on pharmacogenomics for mental health medications. The company developed the GeneSight test, a pharmacogenomic test that analyzes how a patient's genes may affect their response to medications commonly prescribed for depression, anxiety, ADHD, and other psychiatric conditions.

In 2016, Assurex Health was acquired by Myriad Genetics and now operates as Myriad Neuroscience. The GeneSight test continues to be widely used to help clinicians personalize medication selection for patients with mental health conditions, reducing trial-and-error prescribing.

The company was backed by Sequoia Capital and other leading investors before its acquisition.
