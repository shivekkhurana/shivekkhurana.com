## Notes

IV One is a healthcare company focused on intravenous (IV) therapy services and solutions. The company works to improve the quality and efficiency of IV access and delivery in hospital and clinical settings, addressing challenges in IV line management, infection prevention, and patient comfort.

IV access and therapy represent a significant portion of hospital procedures, with millions of IV lines placed annually. Complications including infections, infiltration, and repeated access attempts represent both patient safety concerns and healthcare costs.

The company is backed by TVM Capital Healthcare, an investment firm focused on healthcare companies in the Middle East and emerging markets.
