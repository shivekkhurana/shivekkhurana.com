## Notes

Q32 Bio is a clinical-stage biotechnology company developing therapies that target the complement and cytokine pathways to treat autoimmune, inflammatory, and other serious diseases. The company's approach focuses on modulating these key immune pathways to restore balance in patients with immune-mediated conditions.

Q32's pipeline includes programs targeting multiple autoimmune and inflammatory disorders with significant unmet medical need.
