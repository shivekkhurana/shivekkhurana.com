## Notes

Advaxis (Nasdaq: ADXS) is a clinical-stage biotechnology company focused on the discovery, development, and commercialization of immunotherapies for cancer and infectious diseases. The company's proprietary ADXS platform uses live attenuated Listeria monocytogenes bacteria bioengineered to secrete antigen/adjuvant fusion proteins to stimulate anti-tumor immune responses.

The Lm Technology platform has been shown to activate both innate and adaptive immunity while reducing immunosuppression in the tumor microenvironment.
