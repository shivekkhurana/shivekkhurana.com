## Notes

Corvidia Therapeutics was a biotechnology company focused on developing anti-inflammatory therapies for cardiovascular and kidney diseases. The company's lead program, ziltivekimab, is a monoclonal antibody targeting IL-6 (interleukin-6) to reduce inflammation in patients with chronic kidney disease and cardiovascular risk.

In August 2020, Novo Nordisk acquired Corvidia Therapeutics for up to $2.1 billion to expand its cardiovascular disease pipeline. Ziltivekimab is now being developed by Novo Nordisk in Phase 3 clinical trials.
