## Notes

Selecta Biosciences is a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company developing targeted immunotherapies using its proprietary ImmTOR platform. The company is publicly traded on NASDAQ under the ticker symbol SELB and is headquartered in Watertown, Massachusetts.

The ImmTOR platform uses synthetic vaccine particles (SVP) containing rapamycin to induce antigen-specific immune tolerance. This technology has applications in:
- Enabling repeat dosing of gene therapies by preventing anti-drug antibody formation
- Treating autoimmune diseases
- Improving the efficacy of enzyme replacement therapies

Selecta's lead programs are focused on enabling gene therapy redosing and treating autoimmune conditions.
