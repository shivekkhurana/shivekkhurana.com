## Notes

Modern Health is a leading mental health platform that provides comprehensive mental wellness solutions to employers and their employees worldwide. The platform offers a personalized approach to mental health, combining certified coaching, licensed therapy, self-guided digital programs, and community support.

Founded in 2017, Modern Health has grown rapidly to become one of the most widely used enterprise mental health platforms, serving employees at major companies globally. The company's evidence-based approach helps organizations improve employee wellbeing while reducing healthcare costs.
