## Notes

Mythic Therapeutics is a biotechnology company that has uncovered fundamental mechanisms governing how antibody drugs interact with target cells. The company is leveraging these discoveries to develop enhanced versions of existing FDA-approved antibody therapies with improved efficacy and safety profiles.

Mythic's approach enables the creation of next-generation antibody therapeutics that can overcome limitations of current treatments, potentially providing better outcomes for patients with cancer and other serious diseases.
