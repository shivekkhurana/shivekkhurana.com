## Notes

NanoCellect Biomedical develops innovative microfluidic-based cell sorting solutions that enable researchers to sort viable cells for a wide range of applications. The company's WOLF and CYTO cell sorting systems use gentle microfluidic technology that maintains cell viability better than traditional fluorescence-activated cell sorting (FACS) approaches.

NanoCellect's platforms are used in antibody discovery, cell line development, single-cell omics, and plant and animal genomics research, enabling scientists to isolate specific cell populations while preserving cell health and function.
