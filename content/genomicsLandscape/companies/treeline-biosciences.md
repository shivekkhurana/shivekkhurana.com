## Notes

Treeline Biosciences is a biotechnology company developing precision medicines intended to treat cancer and other serious conditions. The company is headquartered in Cambridge, Massachusetts.

In September 2025, Treeline raised $621.8 million in Series A funding, making it one of the largest Series A rounds in biotechnology history. The round was led by Casdin Capital and Deerfield Management, reflecting strong investor confidence in the company's approach to precision medicine and drug discovery.

The company is leveraging AI and computational approaches to accelerate the discovery and development of targeted therapeutics for patients with cancer and other serious diseases.
