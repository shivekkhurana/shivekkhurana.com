## Notes

Univercells is a biotechnology company focused on making biologics accessible to everyone through innovative manufacturing technologies. The company is headquartered in Gosselies, Belgium.

The company's mission is "Biologics for all" - developing production platforms that dramatically reduce the cost and complexity of manufacturing biological medicines. Univercells' technologies enable:
- Decentralized biomanufacturing
- Reduced capital and operating costs
- Scalable production from clinical to commercial scale
- Applications in vaccines, cell therapies, and other biologics

The company has spun out several businesses focused on specific applications of its manufacturing platform.
