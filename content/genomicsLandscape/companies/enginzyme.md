## Notes

EnginZyme is an industrial biotechnology company headquartered in Stockholm, Sweden, focused on developing enzyme-based manufacturing solutions for the chemical industry. The company's technology enables the sustainable production of chemicals that are currently manufactured using fossil fuel-based processes.

EnginZyme has developed proprietary enzyme immobilization technology that allows biocatalysts to be used in industrial manufacturing processes, enabling cell-free biocatalysis at scale. This approach offers potential advantages in sustainability, cost, and product quality compared to traditional chemical synthesis.

The company is part of the Sofinnova Partners portfolio and represents the growing investment in sustainable chemistry and green manufacturing technologies.
