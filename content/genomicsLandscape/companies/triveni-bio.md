## Notes

Triveni Bio (formerly known as Amagma Therapeutics) is a biotechnology company developing therapeutics for serious diseases. The company is headquartered in Cambridge, Massachusetts.

The company is focused on developing novel therapeutic approaches for cancer and other serious conditions, leveraging innovative drug discovery platforms and mechanisms of action.

Triveni Bio is backed by Polaris Partners, a leading healthcare-focused venture capital firm.
