## Notes

Moderna is a biotechnology pioneer that developed and commercialized mRNA technology for therapeutics and vaccines. The company's mRNA platform enables the rapid design and production of vaccines and therapeutics by instructing cells to produce specific proteins.

Founded in 2010 as a Flagship Pioneering portfolio company, Moderna achieved global recognition with the development of mRNA-1273 (Spikevax), one of the first COVID-19 vaccines authorized worldwide. The company is now expanding its mRNA platform across multiple therapeutic areas including infectious diseases, immuno-oncology, rare diseases, and cardiovascular diseases.
