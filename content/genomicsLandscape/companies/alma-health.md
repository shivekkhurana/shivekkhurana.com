## Notes

Alma Health is a digital mental health company focused on making mental healthcare accessible and affordable across the Middle East and North Africa (MENA) region. The platform connects users with licensed therapists and provides digital tools for mental wellness, addressing the significant mental health treatment gap in the region.

The company offers culturally-sensitive mental health services in Arabic and English, helping to destigmatize mental health care in conservative societies.
