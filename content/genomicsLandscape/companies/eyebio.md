## Notes

EyeBio is a biotechnology company focused on developing therapeutics for eye diseases. Ophthalmology represents a significant market for novel therapeutics, with conditions including age-related macular degeneration, diabetic retinopathy, and various genetic eye diseases affecting millions of patients.

The eye is an attractive target for drug development due to its accessibility for local delivery, immune privilege, and the ability to monitor treatment effects. Companies in this space are developing various modalities including small molecules, biologics, and gene therapies.
