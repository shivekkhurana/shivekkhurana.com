## Notes

ThymoFox is a biotechnology company focused on identifying compounds that induce regeneration and rejuvenation of the thymus. The thymus is a critical organ for immune system function, producing T cells that are essential for adaptive immunity. However, the thymus begins to shrink (involute) after puberty, contributing to age-related immune decline.

ThymoFox is developing therapeutics to restore thymus function, which could:
- Improve immune function in elderly patients
- Enhance recovery from immunosuppressive treatments (chemotherapy, bone marrow transplant)
- Treat age-related immune decline (immunosenescence)
- Potentially improve vaccine responses in older adults

The company is backed by Apollo Health Ventures, which focuses on longevity and aging-related biotechnology.
