## Notes

Solve Therapeutics is a biotechnology company focused on developing next-generation therapeutics for cancer treatment. The company is working on antibody-drug conjugates (ADCs) and other targeted therapeutic modalities.

ADCs combine the targeting specificity of antibodies with the cell-killing potency of cytotoxic drugs, enabling more precise delivery of cancer-killing payloads to tumor cells while minimizing damage to healthy tissue.
