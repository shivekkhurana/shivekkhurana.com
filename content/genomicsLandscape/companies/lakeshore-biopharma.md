## Notes

LakeShore Biopharma is a global biopharmaceutical company dedicated to addressing critical global health needs through the development and scaled manufacturing of vaccines and biologics. The company focuses on bringing essential vaccines to underserved markets and developing new biologic therapies.

With operations in both China and the United States, LakeShore Biopharma combines global reach with local manufacturing capabilities to improve access to critical healthcare products.
