## Notes

Avation Medical is a medical device company focused on developing next-generation neuromodulation therapies for urological conditions. The company's proprietary technology platform is designed to provide effective, minimally invasive treatment options for patients suffering from overactive bladder (OAB), urinary incontinence, and other bladder dysfunction conditions.

The company has received FDA clearance for its tibial nerve stimulation device and is advancing commercialization of its products in the U.S. market.
