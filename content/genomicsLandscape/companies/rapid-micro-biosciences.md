## Notes

Rapid Micro Biosystems is a life sciences technology company that provides automated microbial detection solutions for pharmaceutical and biopharmaceutical manufacturing. The company's Growth Direct system automates sterility and bioburden testing, providing faster results than traditional methods.

The Growth Direct system helps pharmaceutical manufacturers ensure product quality and safety while reducing testing time and improving manufacturing efficiency.
