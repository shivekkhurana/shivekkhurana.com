## Notes

Nouscom is a clinical-stage immuno-oncology company focused on developing novel virus-based immunotherapies for cancer treatment. The company's proprietary platform is designed to generate potent and durable anti-tumor T cell responses using viral vector technology and personalized neoantigen cancer vaccines.

Nouscom's approach combines adenovirus-based vector platforms with personalized cancer vaccine technology to activate the patient's immune system against tumors.
