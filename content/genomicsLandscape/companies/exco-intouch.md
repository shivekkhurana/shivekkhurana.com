## Notes

Exco InTouch is a digital health company based in Nottingham, UK, providing digital engagement and data capture solutions for clinical research and healthcare programs. The company offers technology platforms that enable pharmaceutical companies, CROs, and healthcare organizations to engage patients and capture data throughout clinical trials and real-world studies.

The company's solutions support patient recruitment, retention, and engagement in clinical trials, addressing key challenges in drug development. Their platforms enable electronic patient-reported outcomes (ePRO), electronic consent, and digital biomarker capture.
