## Notes

Mirada Medical is a medical imaging software company specializing in personalized image-guided cancer solutions. The company develops AI-powered tools for radiation oncology, nuclear medicine, and diagnostic imaging that help clinicians plan and deliver more precise cancer treatments.

Based in Oxford, UK, Mirada's software enables automatic contouring, image registration, and treatment response monitoring, helping to improve outcomes in cancer care while reducing clinician workload.
