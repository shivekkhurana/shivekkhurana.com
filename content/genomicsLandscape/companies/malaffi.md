## Notes

Malaffi is the first Health Information Exchange (HIE) platform in the Middle East and North Africa (MENA) region. The platform enables secure sharing of patient health information across Abu Dhabi's healthcare ecosystem, connecting public and private healthcare providers to improve care coordination and patient outcomes.

Part of G42 Healthcare's digital health initiatives, Malaffi supports Abu Dhabi's vision to create an integrated healthcare system with unified electronic health records accessible to authorized healthcare providers.
