## Notes

ImmunoGenesis is a biotechnology company focused on developing novel immunotherapy approaches for cancer treatment. The company is working on technologies to enhance the ability of the immune system to recognize and destroy cancer cells.

Based in the Philadelphia area, ImmunoGenesis is backed by BioAdvance, a life sciences investment organization focused on early-stage companies in the Mid-Atlantic region. The company is developing approaches that may complement or improve upon existing immunotherapy modalities like checkpoint inhibitors and CAR-T cell therapy.
