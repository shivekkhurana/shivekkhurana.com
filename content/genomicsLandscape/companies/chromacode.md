## Notes

ChromaCode is a molecular diagnostics company that has developed High Definition PCR (HDPCR™) technology, which dramatically enhances the multiplexing capabilities of existing PCR instruments. This technology allows laboratories to run more complex, multi-target tests on their current equipment without requiring new hardware investment.

The company's technology is particularly valuable for infectious disease testing, where the ability to test for multiple pathogens simultaneously can improve diagnostic accuracy and efficiency.
