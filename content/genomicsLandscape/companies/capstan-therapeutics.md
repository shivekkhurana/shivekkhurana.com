## Notes

Capstan Therapeutics is a biotechnology company developing in vivo cell programming therapies. The company uses lipid nanoparticle (LNP) technology to deliver genetic instructions directly to cells within the body, enabling the programming of immune cells in vivo without the need for traditional ex vivo cell manufacturing.

This approach aims to overcome key limitations of current cell therapies, including the complexity and cost of manufacturing, and could potentially make cell therapies more accessible to patients. The company is backed by Alexandria Venture Investments and other life sciences investors.
