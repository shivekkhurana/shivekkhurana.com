## Notes

Viz.ai is an AI-powered healthcare platform using artificial intelligence to improve patient care and clinical workflows. The company is headquartered in San Francisco, California.

Viz.ai's platform uses deep learning algorithms to analyze medical images and alert care teams to potential conditions requiring urgent attention. The company's solutions include:
- **Stroke detection**: AI analysis of CT scans to identify large vessel occlusions
- **Cardiovascular disease**: Detection of pulmonary embolism, aortic disease, and other conditions
- **Care coordination**: Automated workflows to connect the right specialists with patients

The company's AI solutions are FDA-cleared and used by hundreds of hospitals across the United States.
