## Notes

AMILI (Asian Microbiome Library Initiative) is Asia's leading microbiome company focused on precision medicine applications. The company has built Asia's largest gut microbiome database and is developing microbiome-based solutions for diagnostics, therapeutics, and personalized nutrition.

The company leverages its Asian-specific microbiome data to develop products tailored to Asian populations, whose gut microbiomes differ significantly from Western populations due to dietary and genetic factors.
