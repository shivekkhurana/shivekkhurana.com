## Notes

Suki is a healthcare AI company developing voice-powered digital assistants aimed at reducing administrative workloads for healthcare providers. Founded in 2017 and headquartered in Redwood City, California, the company has become a leader in AI-powered clinical documentation.

The Suki Assistant uses natural language processing and machine learning to:
- Automatically generate clinical documentation from physician-patient conversations
- Integrate with electronic health record (EHR) systems
- Reduce documentation time by up to 70%
- Allow physicians to spend more time with patients

In October 2024, Suki raised $70 million in Series D funding, valuing the company at approximately $500 million.
