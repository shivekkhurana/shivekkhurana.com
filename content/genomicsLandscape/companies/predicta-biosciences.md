## Notes

Predicta Biosciences is a biotechnology company that utilizes artificial intelligence and multi-omics data analysis to develop personalized diagnostics and treatment recommendations. The company's platform integrates genomic, proteomic, metabolomic, and other omics data to provide comprehensive patient profiles.

By combining multi-omics analysis with AI/ML algorithms, Predicta enables precision medicine approaches that can match patients with optimal treatments.
