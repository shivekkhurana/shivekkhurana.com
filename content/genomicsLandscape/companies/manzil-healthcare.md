## Notes

Manzil Healthcare Services is the leading home healthcare provider in the United Arab Emirates and the broader GCC region. The company delivers comprehensive medical care directly to patients in their homes, including nursing services, physiotherapy, specialized care programs, and medical equipment supplies.

As a portfolio company of TVM Capital Healthcare, Manzil has expanded its services across the region and represents the growing shift toward value-based, patient-centered healthcare delivery in the Middle East.
