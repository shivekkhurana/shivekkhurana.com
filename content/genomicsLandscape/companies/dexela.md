## Notes

Dexela is a medical imaging technology company focused on developing X-ray imaging systems for early detection of breast cancer. The company's technology aims to improve the sensitivity and accuracy of breast cancer screening through advanced detector systems.

Early detection remains critical in improving breast cancer outcomes, and companies like Dexela are working to develop next-generation imaging technologies that can detect cancers at earlier, more treatable stages.

Note: Dexela (as a detector technology brand) was acquired by Varex Imaging Corporation. The current status of Dexela as an independent entity requires further verification.
