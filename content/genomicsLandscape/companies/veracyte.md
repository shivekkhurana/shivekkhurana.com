## Notes

Veracyte is a genomic diagnostics company providing precision medicine solutions through advanced genomic testing. The company is publicly traded on NASDAQ under the ticker symbol VCYT and is headquartered in South San Francisco, California.

Veracyte develops and commercializes genomic tests that help physicians make more informed diagnostic and treatment decisions. The company's tests cover multiple disease areas including:
- Thyroid cancer
- Lung cancer
- Prostate cancer
- Breast cancer
- Idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis

The company's tests use machine learning and genomic analysis to provide actionable insights that can help avoid unnecessary surgeries and guide treatment selection.
