## Notes

KulaOmics is a multi-omics company specializing in genomics and systems biology approaches. The company integrates multiple layers of biological data including genomics, transcriptomics, proteomics, and metabolomics to provide comprehensive insights for research and clinical applications.

As a portfolio company of Corundum Systems Biology, KulaOmics leverages systems-level approaches to understand complex biological processes and develop solutions for precision medicine.
