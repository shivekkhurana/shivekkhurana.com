## Notes

YEARS is a data-driven precision medicine company focused on developing a comprehensive proprietary dataset of health parameters to enable the discovery and validation of novel biomarkers. The company is headquartered in Berlin, Germany.

The company's approach combines:

- Deep phenotyping of health parameters
- Multi-omic data integration
- Advanced analytics and AI
- Longitudinal health tracking

YEARS aims to identify biomarkers that can predict health trajectories and enable earlier intervention for age-related diseases. The company is part of the Apollo Health Ventures portfolio, which focuses on longevity and aging-related biotechnology.
