## Notes

Developing treatments for neurodegenerative diseases, particularly Alzheimer's disease. Led $100 million Series C funding round in July 2024, with Novo Holdings as lead investor. Funds support Phase 2 clinical trials of lead drug candidate ASN51, an O-GlcNAcase (OGA) inhibitor aimed at preventing tau protein aggregation in the brain.
