## Notes

Tagomic is a biotechnology company harnessing the power of multiomics to transform genomic medicine. The company integrates multiple types of biological data - including genomics, transcriptomics, proteomics, and metabolomics - to enable a more comprehensive understanding of disease biology and treatment response.

Multiomics approaches provide a more complete picture of biological systems than single-omic methods, enabling:

- Better disease characterization
- Improved patient stratification
- More accurate treatment selection
- Novel biomarker discovery

Tagomic's platform aims to translate multiomics insights into clinical applications that improve patient outcomes.
