## Notes

DYSIS Medical is a medical device company headquartered in Edinburgh, UK, specializing in advanced imaging technology for cervical cancer detection. The company's flagship product, the DYSIS Colposcope, uses proprietary Dynamic Spectral Imaging (DSI) technology to enhance the detection of cervical pre-cancer and cancer during colposcopy examinations.

The DYSIS system provides objective, color-coded cervical maps that help clinicians identify areas of abnormality that might be missed during standard colposcopy. This technology aims to improve diagnostic accuracy and patient outcomes in cervical cancer screening programs.
