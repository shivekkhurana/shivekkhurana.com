## Notes

Oscar Health is a technology-focused health insurance company that aims to make healthcare more accessible, understandable, and affordable. The company offers individual, family, and small group health insurance plans with a focus on user-friendly technology, telemedicine, and personalized care.

Oscar uses technology to simplify the insurance experience, providing members with easy-to-use mobile apps, concierge health services, and data-driven personalized recommendations.
