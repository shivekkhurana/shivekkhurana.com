## Notes

Emalex Biosciences is a clinical-stage biotechnology company focused on developing treatments for movement disorders and other neurological conditions. The company was founded to address significant unmet needs in conditions such as Tourette syndrome and tardive dyskinesia.

Emalex is part of the Bain Capital Life Sciences portfolio, which focuses on building companies to address significant unmet medical needs. The company is advancing clinical programs targeting movement disorders that significantly impact patient quality of life.
