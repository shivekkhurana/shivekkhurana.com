## Notes

Lightcast Bio is a biotechnology company focused on single-cell functional profiling, measuring function at the individual cell level to advance drug discovery and development. The company's platform enables researchers to understand cellular responses and identify therapeutic targets with unprecedented precision.

By profiling function cell-by-cell, Lightcast Bio provides insights into cellular heterogeneity and functional states that are critical for understanding disease biology and developing effective therapies.
