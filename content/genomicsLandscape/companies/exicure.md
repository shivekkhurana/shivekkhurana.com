## Notes

Exicure was a biotechnology company that developed a proprietary Spherical Nucleic Acid (SNA) technology platform for gene regulation therapeutics. SNAs are nanostructures consisting of nucleic acids arranged in a spherical configuration, designed to enable efficient cellular uptake and gene modulation.

The company was originally based in Chicago and was publicly traded on NASDAQ (XCUR). However, Exicure faced significant business challenges, including clinical setbacks and financial difficulties, leading to restructuring efforts.
