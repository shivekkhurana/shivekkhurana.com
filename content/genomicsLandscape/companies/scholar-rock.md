## Notes

Scholar Rock is a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company focused on developing innovative medicines by modulating protein growth factors. The company is publicly traded on NASDAQ under the ticker symbol SRRK and is headquartered in Cambridge, Massachusetts.

The company's approach targets the activation of latent growth factors, particularly in the TGF-beta superfamily, to treat diseases where these proteins play a fundamental role. Their lead program apitegromab targets myostatin for the treatment of spinal muscular atrophy (SMA).

Scholar Rock's platform enables selective modulation of growth factor signaling, potentially offering more targeted therapeutic effects with improved safety profiles compared to traditional approaches.
