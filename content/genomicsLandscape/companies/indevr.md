## Notes

InDevR is a life science company developing advanced analytical tools for characterizing vaccines and biologics, as well as infectious disease diagnostics. The company's technology platforms include multiplexed microarray assays that can rapidly measure multiple analytes simultaneously.

Based in Boulder, Colorado, InDevR serves pharmaceutical companies, government agencies, and research institutions with products for vaccine quality control, influenza surveillance, and other applications. The company's VaxArray and other platforms provide faster, more accurate testing compared to traditional methods.

InDevR's products have been used for influenza vaccine potency testing, helping manufacturers and regulators ensure vaccine quality and efficacy.
