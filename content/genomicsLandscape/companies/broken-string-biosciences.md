## Notes

Broken String Biosciences is a biotechnology company headquartered in Cambridge, UK, focused on developing advanced DNA break detection technology for safer cell and gene therapies. Their proprietary BREAK-seq platform enables precise mapping of DNA double-strand breaks, helping researchers and pharmaceutical companies identify off-target effects of gene editing tools like CRISPR-Cas9.

The company spun out of the Wellcome Sanger Institute and Cambridge University, leveraging foundational research in DNA damage detection. Their technology is used by gene therapy developers to improve the safety profile of gene editing-based treatments.
