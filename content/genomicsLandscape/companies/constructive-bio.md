## Notes

Constructive Bio is a synthetic biology company pioneering approaches for building entirely synthetic genomes and reprogramming the genetic code of living organisms. Their platform enables programmable polymer synthesis and novel biomanufacturing capabilities.

The company was founded based on groundbreaking research from the MRC Laboratory of Molecular Biology (LMB) in Cambridge, including the first complete synthesis of a designer bacterial genome. Their technology opens new possibilities for creating organisms with expanded genetic codes that can produce novel materials and therapeutics.
