## Notes

Daye (formerly YourDaye) is a women's health company focused on developing innovative products for gynecological health. The company is headquartered in London, United Kingdom.

The company's products include:

- **CBD Tampons**: Tampons infused with CBD to help manage period pain
- **Vaginal Microbiome Screening**: At-home tests to screen for common vaginal infections and STIs
- **Period Pain Treatments**: Various products for menstrual health

Daye aims to improve women's health outcomes by providing accessible, science-backed products that address common but often overlooked gynecological issues.
