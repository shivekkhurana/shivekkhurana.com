## Notes

Transcarent is a consumer-directed health and care platform that helps self-insured employers provide better healthcare experiences for their employees. Founded in 2020 and headquartered in San Francisco, California, the company has rapidly grown to become a significant player in the employer healthcare market.

The platform provides:
- Navigation and care coordination services
- Virtual care and telehealth
- Surgery and specialty care management
- Pharmacy benefits
- Mental health services
- AI-powered health guidance

In April 2025, Transcarent raised $475 million in Series D funding, led by 7wireVentures and General Catalyst.
