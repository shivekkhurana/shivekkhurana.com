## Notes

Dianthus Therapeutics is a clinical-stage biotechnology company focused on developing precision antibody therapeutics targeting the complement system. The complement system is part of the innate immune system and plays a role in various autoimmune and inflammatory diseases.

The company was created through a merger between Dianthus and Magenta Therapeutics' autoimmune disease program. Dianthus is developing DNTH103, a monoclonal antibody targeting C5aR1 (complement component 5a receptor 1), for the treatment of severe autoimmune diseases.

Dianthus is publicly traded on NASDAQ under the ticker symbol DNTH.
