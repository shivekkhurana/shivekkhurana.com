## Notes

Baraya Extended Care is a Saudi Arabian healthcare services company that provides extended care and home healthcare services. The company focuses on delivering quality care to patients who require ongoing medical support after hospitalization or who need long-term care services in their homes or extended care facilities.

As part of the TVM Capital Healthcare portfolio, Baraya is contributing to the development of healthcare infrastructure in the Middle East, aligned with Saudi Arabia's Vision 2030 healthcare transformation goals.
