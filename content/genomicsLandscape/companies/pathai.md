## Notes

PathAI is an AI-powered pathology company that uses machine learning to improve the accuracy and efficiency of disease diagnosis. The company's platform analyzes pathology images to assist pathologists in detecting cancer and other diseases with greater precision and consistency.

PathAI partners with pharmaceutical companies and healthcare institutions to develop and deploy AI-powered diagnostic tools that support clinical decision-making and drug development.
