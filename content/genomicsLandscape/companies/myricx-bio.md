## Notes

Myricx Bio is a biotechnology company specializing in the development of next-generation antibody-drug conjugates (ADCs) for cancer therapy. ADCs combine the targeting precision of antibodies with the cytotoxic power of small molecule drugs, enabling selective destruction of cancer cells while minimizing damage to healthy tissue.

The company's platform focuses on novel linker-payload technologies that improve the stability, efficacy, and safety profile of ADCs compared to earlier generations of these therapeutics.
