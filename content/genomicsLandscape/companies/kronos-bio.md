## Notes

Kronos Bio is a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company focused on developing novel cancer therapies that modulate transcription to address the underlying drivers of disease. The company's approach targets transcription factors, which are proteins that control gene expression and are frequently dysregulated in cancer but have historically been difficult to target with small molecule drugs.

The company went public in October 2020 on NASDAQ under the ticker KRON. Kronos Bio's lead programs focus on targeting MYC and other oncogenic transcription factors.
