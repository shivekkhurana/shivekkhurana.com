## Notes

SonderMind is a mental health technology company that connects individuals with licensed therapists and provides comprehensive mental health services. Founded in 2017 and headquartered in Denver, Colorado, the company has grown to become one of the leading mental health platforms in the United States.

The platform offers:
- Matching individuals with licensed therapists based on their specific needs
- In-person and telehealth therapy sessions
- Insurance navigation and billing support
- Tools for therapists to manage their practices

SonderMind is backed by F-Prime Capital and other leading healthcare investors.
