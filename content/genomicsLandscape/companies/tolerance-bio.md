## Notes

Tolerance Bio is a biotechnology company focused on developing therapeutics that induce immune tolerance. The company is part of the BioAdvance portfolio, a regional life sciences investment organization based in the Philadelphia area.

Immune tolerance approaches aim to reprogram the immune system to stop attacking self-tissues (in autoimmune diseases) or to accept transplanted organs without the need for lifelong immunosuppression. This represents a potentially transformative approach to treating:
- Autoimmune diseases
- Transplant rejection
- Allergies
- Other immune-mediated conditions
