## Notes

Integrated Biosciences is a biotechnology company that combines synthetic biology and machine learning to understand and manipulate cellular stress responses. The company's approach aims to develop therapeutics that can extend healthy lifespan by targeting fundamental mechanisms of cellular aging and stress.

The company's platform integrates high-throughput synthetic biology experiments with AI/ML analysis to discover compounds and pathways that modulate how cells respond to various stressors. This approach has applications in developing treatments for age-related diseases and potentially interventions that affect the aging process itself.

Integrated Biosciences represents a new wave of longevity-focused biotechs that apply cutting-edge computational and experimental technologies to aging research.
