## Notes

Pelagos Pharmaceuticals is developing oral drugs to prevent and treat diseases of aging. The company's approach targets fundamental mechanisms of aging to address multiple age-related conditions including obesity, heart failure, sarcopenia (muscle loss), and neurodegenerative diseases.

As a university spinout, Pelagos leverages academic research to develop novel therapeutics for the growing population affected by age-related diseases.
