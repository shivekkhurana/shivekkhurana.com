## Notes

Deep Apple Therapeutics is a biotechnology company focused on accelerating drug discovery through computational approaches. The company leverages structure-based virtual screening of ultra-large chemical libraries combined with deep learning models to identify promising drug candidates more efficiently than traditional methods.

The company's approach addresses one of the key bottlenecks in pharmaceutical development: the time and cost required to screen vast chemical spaces for potential drug candidates. By using AI and computational methods, Deep Apple Therapeutics aims to dramatically reduce the time from target identification to lead compound discovery.
