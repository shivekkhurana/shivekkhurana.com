## Notes

Ventus Therapeutics is a biotechnology company focused on discovering and developing small-molecule medicines that target innate immune system pathways. The company is headquartered in Waltham, Massachusetts.

The innate immune system is the body's first line of defense against pathogens and plays a key role in inflammation. Dysregulation of innate immune pathways contributes to many diseases, including:
- Autoimmune conditions
- Inflammatory diseases
- Certain cancers
- Neurodegenerative diseases

Ventus is developing small molecules that modulate key innate immune sensors and pathways to treat these conditions.
