## Notes

Replicate Biosciences is a biotechnology company focused on developing self-amplifying RNA (saRNA) therapeutics. Unlike traditional mRNA, self-amplifying RNA contains additional genetic sequences that allow it to replicate within cells, producing more protein from smaller doses.

The company's saRNA platform offers potential advantages over conventional mRNA therapeutics, including lower dosing requirements, extended duration of protein expression, and potentially improved efficacy for certain applications including vaccines and protein replacement therapies.

Replicate Biosciences is part of the Apple Tree Partners portfolio.
