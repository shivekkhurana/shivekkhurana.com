## Notes

COMPASS Pathways (NASDAQ: CMPS) is a mental health care company dedicated to accelerating patient access to evidence-based innovation in mental health. The company is developing COMP360, a proprietary formulation of synthetic psilocybin being studied in clinical trials for treatment-resistant depression (TRD).

COMP360 psilocybin therapy received FDA Breakthrough Therapy designation in 2018, and the company is conducting one of the largest clinical programs with psilocybin in the world. The therapy is administered in conjunction with psychological support.
