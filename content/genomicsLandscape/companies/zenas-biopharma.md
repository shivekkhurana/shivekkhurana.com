## Notes

Zenas BioPharma is a global biopharmaceutical company focused on developing differentiated treatments for autoimmune diseases. The company is headquartered in Waltham, Massachusetts, with operations in multiple regions.

The company is developing a pipeline of novel therapeutics for autoimmune conditions, targeting diseases with significant unmet medical need. Zenas's approach focuses on innovative mechanisms to modulate the immune system and address the underlying causes of autoimmune pathology.

The company has attracted significant investment from leading healthcare investors including Novo Holdings.
