## Notes

Phathom Pharmaceuticals is a biopharmaceutical company focused on developing and commercializing novel treatments for gastrointestinal diseases. The company's lead product, vonoprazan, is a potassium-competitive acid blocker (PCAB) that provides faster and more potent acid suppression than traditional proton pump inhibitors (PPIs).

Vonoprazan is FDA-approved and marketed as VOQUEZNA for erosive esophagitis and VOQUEZNA Triple Pak/Dual Pak for H. pylori infection.
