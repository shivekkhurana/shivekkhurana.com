## Notes

Aptinyx Inc. is a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company focused on developing novel therapies for neurological disorders by modulating NMDA receptor function. The company's proprietary platform enables the development of small molecule NMDA receptor modulators with unique mechanisms of action.

The company's lead programs have targeted neuropathic pain and cognitive impairment, with NYX-2925 being a key drug candidate that advanced through clinical trials. Aptinyx has also explored treatments for fibromyalgia and other conditions involving NMDA receptor dysfunction.

Listed on NASDAQ under the ticker symbol APTX. The company is backed by Bain Capital Life Sciences among other investors.
