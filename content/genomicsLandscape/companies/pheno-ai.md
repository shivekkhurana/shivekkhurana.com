## Notes

Pheno.AI is an AI-powered healthcare platform that uses phenotypic analysis and machine learning to discover biomarkers and accelerate drug development. The company's platform enables comprehensive patient phenotyping by integrating and analyzing diverse health data sources.

Pheno.AI's technology helps pharmaceutical companies identify patient populations, discover biomarkers, and optimize clinical trial design through AI-driven insights.
