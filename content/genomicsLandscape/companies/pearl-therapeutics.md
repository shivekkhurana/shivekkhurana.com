## Notes

Pearl Therapeutics was a biopharmaceutical company focused on developing inhaled therapies for respiratory diseases, particularly chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD). The company developed PT003, a combination therapy of formoterol and glycopyrrolate.

In 2013, AstraZeneca acquired Pearl Therapeutics for up to $1.15 billion ($560 million upfront plus milestones), gaining access to its respiratory pipeline and metered dose inhaler technology.

## Team (at time of acquisition)

- **Don Kellerman, Ph.D.** - Chief Scientific Officer
- Leadership team from respiratory therapeutics backgrounds

## Additional Research Findings

- Founded in 2006 in Redwood City, California
- Portfolio company of 5AM Ventures
- Acquired by AstraZeneca in 2013 for up to $1.15 billion
- Developed PT003 (now Bevespi Aerosphere) for COPD
- Pioneered metered dose inhaler (pMDI) technology
- Focus on combination therapies for respiratory diseases
- Products now part of AstraZeneca's respiratory portfolio
