## Notes

Initial Therapeutics is developing a new class of drugs designed to prevent the formation of difficult-to-drug protein targets. Unlike traditional approaches that target already-formed proteins, Initial's technology intervenes during protein synthesis (cotranslational) to prevent disease-causing proteins from ever being made.

This approach could address targets that have been considered "undruggable" by conventional small molecules, antibodies, or even newer protein degradation approaches like PROTACs. The cotranslational degradation mechanism targets proteins while they are being synthesized on ribosomes.

The company represents a new frontier in protein homeostasis drug discovery, complementing other degradation approaches in the industry.
