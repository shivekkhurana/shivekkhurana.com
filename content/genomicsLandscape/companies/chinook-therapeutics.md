## Notes

Chinook Therapeutics was a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company dedicated to developing precision medicines for kidney diseases, including IgA nephropathy and other serious kidney conditions. The company was formed through the merger of Chinook Therapeutics and Aduro Biotech in 2020.

In August 2023, Novartis acquired Chinook Therapeutics for approximately $3.5 billion, gaining access to Chinook's kidney disease pipeline, including atrasentan and zigakibart (BION-1301).
