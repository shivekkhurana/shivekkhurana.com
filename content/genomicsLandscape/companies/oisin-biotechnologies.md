## Notes

Oisín Biotechnologies is pioneering genetic medicines to combat sarcopenia (age-related muscle loss) and other age-related diseases. The company's lead candidate aims to increase skeletal muscle mass and strength without exercise, potentially benefiting older populations and those recovering from illnesses or surgeries.

Oisín's approach targets the fundamental biological mechanisms of aging, seeking to develop therapies that can address multiple age-related conditions simultaneously.
