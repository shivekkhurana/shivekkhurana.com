## Notes

OrbusNeich Medical is a global medical device company focused on developing and manufacturing innovative cardiovascular intervention products. The company's portfolio includes coronary stents, drug-coated balloons, and other interventional cardiology devices.

OrbusNeich is known for its bioresorbable stent technology and drug-eluting stents used in treating coronary artery disease.
