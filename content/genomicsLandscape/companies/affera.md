## Notes

Affera was a medical device company focused on developing next-generation cardiac mapping and ablation technology for treating cardiac arrhythmias. The company developed the Sphere-9 catheter with integrated mapping and ablation capabilities, designed to simplify and improve the efficiency of cardiac ablation procedures.

**Acquired by Medtronic in August 2023** for approximately $925 million, with potential for additional milestone payments. The acquisition expanded Medtronic's cardiac ablation solutions portfolio, particularly in pulsed field ablation (PFA) technology.

- Technology now integrated into Medtronic's Cardiac Ablation Solutions business
