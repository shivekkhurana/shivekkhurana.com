## Notes

Alina Vision is a leading ophthalmology and eye care services company operating across the Middle East and North Africa (MENA) region. The company provides comprehensive eye care services including laser vision correction (LASIK), cataract surgery, retina treatments, and general ophthalmology.

Backed by TVM Capital Healthcare, Alina Vision is building a network of eye care centers to address the growing demand for quality ophthalmology services in the region.
