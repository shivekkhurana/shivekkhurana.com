## Notes

neurocare group is a global healthcare company specializing in personalized, evidence-based treatments for mental health conditions. The company operates clinics and provides therapies including transcranial magnetic stimulation (TMS), neurofeedback, and other neuromodulation technologies.

neurocare focuses on treating conditions such as depression, ADHD, anxiety, and other neurological disorders using non-invasive brain stimulation and neurofeedback approaches.
