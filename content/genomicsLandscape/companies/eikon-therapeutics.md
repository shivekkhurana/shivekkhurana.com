## Notes

Eikon Therapeutics is a biotechnology company based in Hayward, California, founded by Nobel laureate Eric Betzig. The company has developed a revolutionary drug discovery platform that combines super-resolution microscopy with artificial intelligence to observe and analyze protein behavior in living cells at unprecedented resolution.

Eikon's technology allows researchers to watch proteins move and interact in real-time within cells, providing insights into biology that were previously impossible to obtain. This approach enables the discovery of novel drug targets and the development of therapeutics for diseases including cancer.

The company has raised substantial funding, including a $350.7 million Series D round in 2025, making it one of the most well-funded private biotech companies.
