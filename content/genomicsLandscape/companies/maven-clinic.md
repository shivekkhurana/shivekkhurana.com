## Notes

Maven Clinic is the leading virtual clinic for women's and family health, offering comprehensive digital healthcare services spanning fertility, pregnancy, parenting, pediatrics, and menopause. The platform connects members with a network of healthcare providers including OB-GYNs, midwives, mental health specialists, and career coaches.

Founded in 2014, Maven has become a unicorn valued at $1.7 billion following its October 2024 funding round. The company serves enterprise clients by providing family benefits programs to their employees, helping companies attract and retain talent while improving healthcare outcomes.
