## Notes

DEBx Medical is a medical technology company based in Belgium focused on developing innovative treatments for chronic wounds. The company's technology uses electrochemical debridement to treat difficult-to-heal wounds, including diabetic foot ulcers, pressure ulcers, and venous leg ulcers.

The company's approach aims to provide a more effective and less painful alternative to traditional wound debridement methods. Chronic wounds represent a significant healthcare burden, particularly in aging populations and patients with diabetes.

DEBx Medical is part of the TVM Capital Healthcare portfolio, which focuses on healthcare investments in growth markets.
