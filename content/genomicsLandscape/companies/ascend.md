## Notes

Ascend Advanced Therapies is a contract development and manufacturing organization (CDMO) focused on supporting the development and manufacturing of cell and gene therapies. The company provides end-to-end services for viral vector production, cell therapy manufacturing, and related process development.

Ascend offers manufacturing services for adeno-associated virus (AAV) vectors, lentiviral vectors, and other gene delivery systems used in gene therapy applications. The company serves pharmaceutical and biotechnology clients developing advanced therapies for genetic diseases, cancer, and other conditions.

In 2024, Ascend merged with ABL (formerly Advanced Bioscience Laboratories) to create an integrated U.S. hub for viral vector manufacturing, combining Ascend's cell and gene therapy expertise with ABL's extensive viral vector experience.

Ascend is a portfolio company of Abingworth, a leading life sciences investment firm.
