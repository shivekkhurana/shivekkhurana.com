## Notes

Metsera is a biotechnology company focused on developing innovative therapies for obesity and metabolic diseases. The company is part of the rapidly growing market for weight loss therapeutics, developing next-generation treatments that aim to improve upon current GLP-1 receptor agonists with better efficacy, tolerability, and convenience.

With significant venture backing including a $290 million Series D round in April 2024, Metsera is well-positioned to advance its pipeline of metabolic disease therapeutics through clinical development.
