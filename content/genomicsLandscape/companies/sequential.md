## Notes

Sequential is a biotechnology company focused on skin microbiome testing and analysis. Known as "The Skin Microbiome Testing Company," Sequential develops diagnostic solutions that analyze the skin microbiome to provide personalized insights for skin health.

The company's technology enables understanding of the complex microbial communities living on human skin and how they relate to various skin conditions. This information can be used to develop personalized skincare recommendations and treatments.

Sequential is a portfolio company of Corundum Systems Biology.
