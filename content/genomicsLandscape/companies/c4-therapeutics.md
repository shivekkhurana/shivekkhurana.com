## Notes

C4 Therapeutics (NASDAQ: CCCC) is a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company pioneering a new generation of medicines based on targeted protein degradation (TPD). The company's proprietary TORPEDO® (Target ORiented Protein Degrader Optimizer) platform enables the development of small molecules called degraders that selectively destroy disease-causing proteins.

The company was founded in 2015 based on foundational research from Harvard, MIT, and Dana-Farber Cancer Institute. C4 Therapeutics went public in 2020 and has established partnerships with major pharmaceutical companies including Roche, Biogen, Betta Pharma, and Merck KGaA.
