## Notes

Pulmocide is developing novel inhaled therapies for patients suffering from serious acute and chronic respiratory diseases. The company's approach focuses on delivering drugs directly to the lungs through inhalation, particularly targeting pulmonary fungal infections that affect immunocompromised patients.

Pulmocide's lead programs address serious respiratory conditions including pulmonary aspergillosis, which affects patients with weakened immune systems.
