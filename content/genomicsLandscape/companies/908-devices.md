## Notes

908 Devices is a pioneering analytical devices company that develops and manufactures portable and desktop mass spectrometry solutions. The company's technology enables real-time chemical and biomolecular analysis in the field, clinic, or lab. Their products serve applications in life sciences research, pharmaceutical development, forensics, and industrial quality control.

Publicly traded on Nasdaq (ticker: MASS) since December 2020.
