## Notes

Pangaea Data is an AI-powered healthcare data company that uses natural language processing (NLP) and machine learning to extract valuable insights from unstructured medical records. The company's platform enables pharmaceutical companies, biotech firms, and healthcare organizations to access real-world data for drug development, clinical trial optimization, and precision medicine.

Pangaea's technology can process millions of patient records to identify patterns, patient cohorts, and outcomes data that would be impossible to extract manually.
