## Notes

LimmaTech Biologics is a clinical-stage biotechnology company focused on developing novel vaccines and biologics to prevent and treat infectious diseases and cancer. The company leverages its proprietary glycoengineering and bioconjugate platform technologies to create next-generation vaccines.

Based in Basel, Switzerland, LimmaTech is developing therapies targeting bacterial infections, including those caused by antimicrobial-resistant pathogens, as well as oncology applications.
