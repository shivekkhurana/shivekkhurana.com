## Notes

Prime Medicine is a leading gene editing company advancing prime editing, a revolutionary gene editing technology that can make virtually any type of DNA edit with high precision. Prime editing was developed at the Broad Institute by Dr. David Liu and licensed to Prime Medicine.

Unlike traditional CRISPR, prime editing can perform precise insertions, deletions, and all 12 types of point mutations without requiring double-strand breaks or donor DNA templates, offering a potentially safer and more versatile approach to genetic medicines.
