## Investment Thesis

Hedosophia invests in growth-stage technology companies that are transforming their industries. Their healthcare investments focus on technology-enabled healthcare companies, particularly those using AI to improve healthcare delivery and outcomes. They often partner with Social Capital for SPAC transactions.
