## Investment Thesis

Founders Fund invests in companies building transformative technologies that others may consider too ambitious or unconventional. They seek founders tackling hard problems with breakthrough approaches. In healthcare and life sciences, they focus on companies using technology to fundamentally reimagine drug discovery, diagnostics, and longevity research.
