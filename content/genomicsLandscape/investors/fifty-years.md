## Investment Thesis

Fifty Years invests in early-stage companies tackling the world's most pressing problems, with a focus on biotechnology, climate technology, and deep tech. They seek founders building technologies that can have positive impact at scale over the next fifty years, taking a long-term, mission-aligned approach.
