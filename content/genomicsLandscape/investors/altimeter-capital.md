## Investment Thesis

Altimeter Capital invests in transformative technology companies at growth stage, with expertise in both public and private markets. In healthcare, they focus on technology-enabled healthcare companies that can achieve significant scale and market impact. They are known for taking concentrated positions in high-conviction investments.
