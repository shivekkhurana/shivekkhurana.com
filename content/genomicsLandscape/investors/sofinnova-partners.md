## Investment Thesis

Sofinnova Partners invests in innovative life sciences companies across Europe and globally. They focus on companies developing breakthrough therapies, medical technologies, and digital health solutions with strong scientific foundations and clear paths to clinical development. Their experienced team brings deep scientific expertise and extensive industry networks to support portfolio companies.
