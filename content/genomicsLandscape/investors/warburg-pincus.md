## Investment Thesis

Warburg Pincus invests in growth-oriented companies across sectors with a focus on identifying transformational opportunities. In healthcare, they seek companies with differentiated technologies, large addressable markets, and strong management teams. They provide patient capital and operational support to help companies scale globally.
