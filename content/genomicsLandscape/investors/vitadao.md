## Investment Thesis

VitaDAO funds early-stage longevity research that traditional funding sources often overlook. They focus on projects targeting the fundamental mechanisms of aging with potential for significant healthspan extension. Their decentralized model enables rapid, community-driven funding decisions using IP-NFTs (Intellectual Property Non-Fungible Tokens) for research commercialization.
