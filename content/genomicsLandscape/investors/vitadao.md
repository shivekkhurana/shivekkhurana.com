## Investment Thesis

VitaDAO is a decentralized autonomous organization (DAO) that funds early-stage longevity research through community governance. Uses IP-NFTs for research funding and commercialization.

## Notes

- Decentralized autonomous organization (DAO) for longevity research
- Closed $4.1 million fundraising round in January 2023
- Investors include: Pfizer Ventures, Shine Capital, L1 Digital, Beaker DAO, Spaceship DAO, Balaji Srinivasan, Joe Betts-LaCroix
- Operates using native VITA token for governance
- First pharmaceutical company (Pfizer Ventures) to invest in a DAO
- Decentralized science (DeSci) organization
