## Investment Thesis

SoftBank Vision Fund invests in transformative technology companies that can achieve massive scale. In healthcare, they focus on companies leveraging AI, data, and technology platforms to transform drug discovery, healthcare delivery, and patient outcomes. They seek category-defining companies with the potential to become global leaders.
