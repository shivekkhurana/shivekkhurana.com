## Investment Thesis

TMC Venture Fund invests in early-stage healthcare companies that can benefit from the Texas Medical Center ecosystem. They focus on companies developing innovative therapies, medical devices, diagnostics, and digital health solutions. Portfolio companies gain access to clinical expertise, patient populations, and partnership opportunities across TMC member institutions.
