## Investment Thesis

aMoon invests in transformative healthcare and life sciences companies globally, with particular expertise in Israeli innovation. They focus on companies with breakthrough technologies that can significantly improve patient outcomes, leveraging their deep network in the Israeli tech and life sciences ecosystem.
