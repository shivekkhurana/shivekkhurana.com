## Investment Thesis

T. Rowe Price's growth equity strategy focuses on identifying high-quality companies with strong fundamentals and long-term growth potential. In healthcare and life sciences, they invest in companies with differentiated technologies, large market opportunities, and clear paths to profitability. They often serve as anchor investors in late-stage private rounds and IPOs.
