## Investment Thesis

Lightspeed partners with exceptional founders at every stage, from inception to IPO and beyond. In healthcare, they focus on companies leveraging technology to transform healthcare delivery, drug discovery, and diagnostics, with particular interest in AI-powered health solutions and next-generation therapeutics.
