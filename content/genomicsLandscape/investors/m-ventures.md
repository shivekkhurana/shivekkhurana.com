## Investment Thesis

M Ventures invests in innovative companies at the forefront of scientific discovery. In healthcare, they focus on therapeutics, diagnostics, and life science tools that have the potential to transform patient care and accelerate scientific research. They bring strategic partnership opportunities with Merck alongside financial investment.
