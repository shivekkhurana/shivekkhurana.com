## Investment Thesis

Flagship Pioneering conceives and creates companies based on breakthrough scientific ideas that can transform human health and sustainability. Their "Pioneering" model develops concepts internally through a rigorous exploration process, then builds companies with dedicated teams and significant resources from inception.
