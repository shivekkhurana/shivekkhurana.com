## Investment Thesis

SV Health Investors backs exceptional entrepreneurs building transformative healthcare companies. They invest across the healthcare spectrum from early-stage to growth, with deep expertise in life sciences, medical technology, and digital health. Their transatlantic presence provides portfolio companies with access to capital, talent, and partners on both sides of the Atlantic.
