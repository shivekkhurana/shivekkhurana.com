## Investment Thesis

Kleiner Perkins invests in breakthrough technology companies across sectors. Their healthcare investments focus on companies that can fundamentally transform patient care through innovative therapeutics, digital health solutions, and healthcare technology. They bring deep operational expertise and an unparalleled network to portfolio companies.
