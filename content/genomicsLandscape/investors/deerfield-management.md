## Investment Thesis

Deerfield invests across the healthcare ecosystem, from early-stage therapeutics to established biopharma companies. They combine deep scientific expertise with financial discipline to identify companies developing transformative therapies. Their full-lifecycle approach allows them to support companies from seed funding through commercial success.
