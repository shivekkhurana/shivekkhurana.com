## Investment Thesis

Andera Partners' BioDiscovery funds invest in European life sciences companies developing innovative therapeutics, diagnostics, and medical technologies. They focus on companies with strong scientific foundations and clear paths to clinical and commercial success, supporting entrepreneurs from early stage through growth.
