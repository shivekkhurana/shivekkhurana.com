## Investment Thesis

Ysios Capital invests in innovative life sciences companies with breakthrough potential, focusing primarily on European biotech and healthcare companies. They seek companies with differentiated science, strong intellectual property, and experienced management teams that can develop transformative therapies and technologies. The firm takes an active role in company building, providing strategic guidance and leveraging their extensive network in the pharmaceutical industry.
