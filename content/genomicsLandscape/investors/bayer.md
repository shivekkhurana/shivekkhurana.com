## Investment Thesis

Leaps by Bayer invests in companies developing breakthrough solutions in health and agriculture, focusing on technologies that can fundamentally improve human health and nutrition. They take bold bets on transformative science including gene editing, regenerative medicine, and novel therapeutic modalities.
