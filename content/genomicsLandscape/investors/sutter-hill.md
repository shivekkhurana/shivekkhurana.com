## Investment Thesis

Sutter Hill Ventures partners with exceptional entrepreneurs building transformative technology companies. They take a long-term approach, often investing from inception and supporting companies through multiple rounds of financing. In healthcare, they focus on companies leveraging technology and data to transform healthcare delivery and life sciences research.
