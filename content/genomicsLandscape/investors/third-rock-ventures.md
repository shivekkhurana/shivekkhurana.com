## Investment Thesis

Third Rock specializes in creating and building life sciences companies from the ground up. They identify promising scientific opportunities, assemble world-class teams, and provide the capital and strategic support needed to translate breakthrough science into transformative medicines. Their hands-on approach includes incubating companies in their Boston offices.
