## Investment Thesis

Silver Lake invests in leading technology and technology-enabled companies across sectors. In healthcare, they focus on companies at the intersection of technology and healthcare services, including healthcare IT, digital health platforms, and technology-enabled healthcare services. They seek large-scale transformation opportunities with established market leaders.
