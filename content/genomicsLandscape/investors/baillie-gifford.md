## Investment Thesis

Baillie Gifford takes an extremely long-term investment approach (typically 5-10+ years), focusing on identifying companies with potential for transformational growth. In healthcare and life sciences, they invest in companies developing breakthrough therapies and diagnostic technologies that can meaningfully improve patient outcomes at scale.
