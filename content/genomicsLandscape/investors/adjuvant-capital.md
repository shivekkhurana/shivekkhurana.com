## Investment Thesis

Adjuvant Capital invests in companies developing innovative solutions for global health challenges, with a focus on vaccines, infectious diseases, and neglected diseases. As an impact-first investor, they seek to achieve both financial returns and measurable global health impact, particularly for underserved populations.
