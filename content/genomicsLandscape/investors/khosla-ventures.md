## Investment Thesis

Khosla Ventures backs entrepreneurs working on breakthrough technologies that can have massive positive impact. In healthcare, they focus on companies using AI, genomics, and novel approaches to fundamentally reimagine drug discovery, diagnostics, and healthcare delivery.
