## Investment Thesis

Apollo Health Ventures focuses exclusively on longevity biotechnology, investing in companies that target the fundamental mechanisms of aging to prevent and treat age-related diseases. They take a thesis-driven approach based on the biology of aging and partner with leading scientists in the field.
