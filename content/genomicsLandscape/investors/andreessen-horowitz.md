## Investment Thesis

Andreessen Horowitz Bio+Health invests in companies at the intersection of biology and technology, focusing on digital health, therapeutics, and healthcare infrastructure. They believe in the "software eating healthcare" thesis and back founders applying technology to transform healthcare delivery, drug discovery, and patient outcomes.
