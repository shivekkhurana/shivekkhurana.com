## Investment Thesis

SR One invests in innovative life sciences companies developing transformative therapies that can significantly impact patient outcomes. With roots in big pharma, they bring deep scientific expertise and industry connections to portfolio companies. They focus on novel biology, differentiated therapeutic approaches, and experienced management teams.
