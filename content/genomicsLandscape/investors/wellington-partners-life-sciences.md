## Investment Thesis

Wellington Partners invests in European life sciences companies with breakthrough science and strong intellectual property. They focus on companies developing novel therapeutics, medical devices, and diagnostics that address significant unmet medical needs. The firm provides hands-on support and leverages their extensive industry networks to help portfolio companies succeed.
