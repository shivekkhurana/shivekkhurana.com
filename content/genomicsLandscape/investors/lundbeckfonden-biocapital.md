## Investment Thesis

Lundbeckfonden BioCapital invests in innovative life sciences companies with a particular focus on neuroscience, reflecting their deep expertise in the field. They support companies from early stage through growth, bringing patient capital and scientific expertise from the Lundbeck Foundation ecosystem.
