## Investment Thesis

7wireVentures invests in companies that are transforming healthcare through technology, focusing on improving consumer engagement, reducing costs, and enhancing outcomes. They leverage their deep operating experience in healthcare to identify and support companies with the potential to create systemic change in healthcare delivery.
