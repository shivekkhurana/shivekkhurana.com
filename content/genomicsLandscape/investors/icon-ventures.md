## Investment Thesis

Icon Ventures invests in growth-stage companies that are using technology to transform large industries. In healthcare, they focus on companies leveraging technology to improve healthcare access, outcomes, and efficiency, with investments spanning digital health, healthcare services, and enterprise healthcare software.
