## Investment Thesis

V-Bio Ventures invests in European life sciences companies developing breakthrough technologies with strong scientific foundations. They focus on companies emerging from leading research institutions, with particular expertise in companies spun out of VIB and other Flemish research centers. The firm provides strategic support and access to their extensive network of industry partners.
