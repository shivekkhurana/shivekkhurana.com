## Investment Thesis

TPG Biotech invests in leading biotechnology and healthcare companies with differentiated science, experienced management teams, and large market opportunities. They leverage TPG's global resources and operational expertise to help portfolio companies scale. The platform focuses on growth-stage investments where they can provide significant capital and strategic value.
